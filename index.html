Questions

Before you start.

Who pays Duran Yachts?

The buyer. Advisory is billed hourly, and if you buy through Duran Yachts as your buyer's broker, the fees are credited toward the purchase. Working for the buyer only keeps our incentives aligned with yours.

Should I buy a catamaran or a monohull?

It depends on the journey: where you'll sail, with how many people, for how long and how you want to live aboard. We define that first, then match the boat to it.

Can you help me buy a boat in Europe if I live in the Americas?

Yes. Salva is a Florida-licensed yacht broker and a US and Spanish citizen, and works on boats in the US, the Caribbean and the Mediterranean.

Is it a good time to buy a used catamaran?

Today's market favors buyers. Knowing which boats are priced right, which aren't, and why, is what turns that into real savings.

Can you help me buy several boats to start a charter company?

Yes. We advise on fleet selection, multi-boat negotiation and charter-business fit, using long-standing industry contacts.
